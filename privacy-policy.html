Overview

About this policy

I am Pritam Laskar, a freelance developer based in Bangkok, Thailand. I build custom apps and automations for businesses, and some of them connect to Google services through Google APIs (for example Google Sheets, Drive, Calendar, Gmail or YouTube). This policy explains how those apps handle data. It applies to the apps and API integrations I build and run, for clients and for my own business tools.

01

Who decides what data an app handles

Each app is built for a specific purpose agreed with the client who commissions it. The client decides which Google account and data the app works with, and I process that data only to build, run and support the app. I do not use it for any purpose of my own.

02

What an app may access

An app can only reach data that the person signing in approves on Google’s own consent screen, and only within the permissions (“scopes”) shown there. I request the minimum scopes an app needs. Depending on the app, that can include:

  • spreadsheet, document or file contents the app is set up to read or write;
  • calendar events or contact details the app is set up to read or create;
  • email messages or labels, only where the app’s purpose requires it;
  • YouTube channel and video information, and uploading or scheduling videos on a channel the user owns.

I do not ask for access that an app does not need, and an app never reads data from Google services it was not set up to use.

03

How the data is used

  • Only to provide the features of that app to the person who authorised it.
  • It is not sold, rented or used for advertising, profiling or marketing.
  • It is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.
  • No one, including me, reads the content unless needed to support the user at their request, to investigate abuse or security problems, or to comply with the law.

04

Sharing

I do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as needed to provide the app’s features (for example cloud hosting, or an automation or AI service the client has approved for that app), to follow the law, or as part of a business transfer that keeps these protections. Any such provider is used only to run the feature, and I prefer settings that stop providers from keeping or training on the data where that option exists.

05

Google API Services

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Apps that use YouTube API Services are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

06

Storage and security

  • Sign-in tokens and keys are kept as encrypted secrets in private, access-controlled environments, never in public code or shared documents.
  • Data moves over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.
  • Access is limited to the people who need it to run the app, and the apps request the smallest permissions that work.

No system is perfectly secure, but I take reasonable steps to protect the data an app handles.

07

Retention and deletion

Data an app receives is kept only for as long as the app needs it to work, or as agreed with the client. When an engagement ends, or on request, I delete the stored tokens and any copies of data I hold. Any user can revoke an app’s access to their Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

08

Children

These apps are business tools and are not directed at children.

09

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
