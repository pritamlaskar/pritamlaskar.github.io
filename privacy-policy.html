Privacy Policy

YouTube Scheduler · Effective 23 November 2026 · Last updated 23 November 2026

YouTube Scheduler (“the app”) is a small personal tool built and operated by Pritam Laskar (“I”, “me”). It uploads videos to, and schedules them on, my own YouTube channel, The Karobaar Co. It is not offered to the public, and I am its only user.

What the app accesses

When I sign in with Google and approve access, the app can use the YouTube Data API on my channel to:

  • read basic information about my channel (name, handle, counts) to confirm the connection;
  • upload video files that I choose, together with the title, description, tags and category I provide;
  • set or change the publish time and details of my own videos, and read their upload and privacy status.

The app does not read other people’s data, comments, messages, contacts, email or any other Google service.

What is stored, and where

  • A Google sign-in token (an OAuth refresh token) is kept on my own computer and as an encrypted secret in a private code repository, so that scheduled uploads can run automatically.
  • A small log of video IDs that have already been uploaded, so nothing is uploaded twice.

The app does not run analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not collect personal information from anyone else.

Sharing

I do not sell, rent, transfer or share Google user data with anyone. Data goes only between my own systems and Google/YouTube.

Google API Services

The app’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking it makes the stored sign-in token useless; I can also delete the token and the upload log on request. Videos already published on YouTube are managed in YouTube Studio.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
