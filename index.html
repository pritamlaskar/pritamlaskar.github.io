My story

I believe the best teams are human plus machine.

I’m Pritam. I started out as the person who noticed how much of a team’s week disappears into tasks a machine could do. Then AI arrived and everything changed. Suddenly the machine could read, write, sort and decide, not just repeat.

So I went deep. Working in data and operations at Tesco Ireland, Centra, the United Nations and a YC backed startup taught me how work really flows. Building with n8n, Python and Replit taught me what AI is brilliant at, and where it still needs a human.

My belief is simple. Automation and AI should not replace people, they should multiply them. Take away the copy paste, the chasing and the checking, and your team gets back to judgement, creativity and relationships, the things humans do best.

Humans decide.Machines do the doing.
Automate the boring.Keep the judgement calls.
Always explainable.If you can’t follow it, you can’t trust it.

I studied Information Systems at University College Dublin on a scholarship, and I’m still exploring. Every month there is a new model or tool that lets a small team reach like a big one, and I love finding where it genuinely helps.

Questions

Things owners usually ask.

Do I need to be technical?

No. I explain everything in plain language, and you never have to touch code. If you can use email and a spreadsheet, you can use what I build.

You have a UI/UX portfolio, so why are you into automation?

Because they solve the same problem from two ends. UI/UX taught me to start with the person who is stuck. Automation lets me remove the friction behind the screen, not just make it look better. Together I can design the experience and build the engine, so the whole solution actually works for your team.

What if I already use certain tools?

That’s the usual case. I connect the tools you already have, such as your CRM, spreadsheets, email, website and chat, rather than asking you to switch.

Will AI make mistakes?

Sometimes, which is why I design every AI step with checks and a person approving anything important. You get the speed without the risk.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the job. After our first call I give you a clear timeline and scope in writing, so you know what to expect before you commit.

What happens after it’s built?

You get written instructions, a walkthrough for your team and alerts if anything stops working, so it doesn’t depend on me being around.

Is my data safe?

I work with the access you give me and no more, follow clear data handling rules and keep backups. I’m happy to sign an NDA before we start.
