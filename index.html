I solve the problem
behind the problem.

Most people come to me thinking they need a website, a campaign, or a few nice visuals. What they usually need first is clarity, on what they're actually trying to say, to whom, and why it matters. I'm Pritam. I research the problem, build the strategy, and then execute it.

Featured Strategic Project
Oklahoma Targeted Campaign — Strategic Content Concept

A self-directed strategic concept built around Saga Education, a nonprofit that partners with school districts on high-impact tutoring. Saga did not commission this campaign; it's shown here as evidence of how I think about buyer-specific, evidence-led communication for mission-driven organizations.

The Problem
Nonprofits like Saga sell into school districts, where the real buyer is a superintendent or administrator, not a general audience, and generic social content rarely reaches or moves them.
The Audience
Oklahoma district-level decision-makers weighing tutoring investments against measurable learning-loss and staffing pressures in their own schools.
The Insight
Oklahoma's specific education conditions, and the district-level problems they create, needed to be named explicitly before Saga's solution would feel relevant instead of generic.
The Strategy
Research state-level education data first, then structure the campaign as a deliberate progression: problem → evidence → solution, mapped to what a district decision-maker actually weighs.
The Execution
A 10-card LinkedIn carousel connecting Oklahoma-specific education data to district-level problems, then to Saga's tutoring model, card by card.
The Outcome
A reusable buyer-specific campaign framework, proof of research-led, evidence-first content for a mission-driven, trust-sensitive audience.
