How it works

From brief to handover,
without the back-and-forth.

  1. 01

    Brief

    A free intro call. We cover audience, goal and taste, so I leave with a clear picture of what “good” looks like for you.

About

Shaped by real audiences.

I’m Pritam, and I got into design by asking one question: why does one thing hold people’s attention while another gets ignored? I’ve been chasing the answer ever since.

It began with an internship at Outlook, one of India’s top three magazines, with around 1.5 million readers a week. I designed for social media there, where a design has about a second to earn someone’s attention.

Next came United Nations video production for SODEIT, a Cameroon-based organisation, where the job was telling a story clearly to people far from where it was made.

I was a scholarship recipient at University College Dublin, Ireland, and I joined Tesco Ireland as a Digital Experience Designer, improving how shoppers use the Tesco app so everyday tasks felt quick and easy.

Across all of it, one thing stayed true: good design starts with the person looking at it. Today I bring that to brands as a freelance designer, across Canva, the Adobe suite, Figma and AI tools.
